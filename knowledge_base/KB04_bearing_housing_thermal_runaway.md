# Technical Diagnosis Manual: Bearing Housing Thermal Runaway & Mechanical Preload Friction

**Document ID:** KB-PUMP-004  
**Target Asset:** Industrial Centrifugal Slurry / Water Pump  
**Subsystem:** Bearing Bracket Housing & End Covers  
**Severity Level:** High (Risk of Thermal Seizure)

---

## 1. Diagnostic Symptoms & Sensor Telemetry Profile
- **Bearing Housing Temperature:** Exceeds **75.0°C**, climbing steadily towards **82.0°C – 88.0°C**.
- **Vibration Velocity RMS:** Moderately elevated (**2.2 – 3.8 mm/s**), but remains strictly below the ISO 10816 Zone D critical boundary (4.5 mm/s).
- **Discharge Pressure:** Nominal steady operation (**4.2 – 5.8 bar**).
- **Temperature vs. Vibration Balance:** Severe thermal anomaly dominates with only minor mechanical roughness.

---

## 2. Physical Root Causes
1. **Excessive Axial / Radial Preload:** Bearing outer ring pinched in housing due to missing or inadequate thermal expansion shims.
2. **Seal Rubbing Friction:** Defective mechanical labyrinth seal or lip seal installed cocked and rubbing against the rotating shaft sleeve.
3. **Over-greasing Churning:** Excessive grease filling 100% of bearing internal free volume causing hydraulic fluid drag.
4. **Thermal Expansion Differential:** Lack of floating bearing clearance on the non-drive end, restricting axial shaft elongation.

---

## 3. Immediate Diagnostic Verification Checks
1. **Shaft Seal Touch Test:** Use an infrared thermometer to verify whether the seal gland is hotter than the main bearing housing (indicates seal rubbing).
2. **Grease Fill Level:** Check if grease has been recently added by maintenance technicians. Check if housing relief plug is open.
3. **Axial Float Check:** Following shutdown, uncouple pump and check for free axial movement of the non-drive-end floating bearing.
4. **End-Cover Bolt Torque:** Check torque on bearing cap bolts; uneven over-torquing can distort bearing outer race.

---

## 4. Recommended Corrective Maintenance Actions
1. **Open Grease Relief:** Remove the lower grease relief plug and run pump for 30 minutes to allow excess grease to purge automatically.
2. **Inspect Seal Gland Clearance:** Realign and re-shim seal end cover if metal-to-metal rubbing is present.
3. **Verify Bearing Internal Clearance:** Ensure correct C3 or C4 radial internal clearance is selected when replacing bearings on hot-service pumps.
4. **Cooling Jacket Flush:** If pump is equipped with a bearing bracket water cooling chamber, check for sediment blockage in cooling lines.

---

## 5. Safety Warnings & LOTO Notes
- **WARNING:** Hot oil/grease can erupt from relief ports under pressure. Wear full face shield and heat-resistant PPE when removing plugs.
- **LOTO:** Adhere to plant electrical and mechanical isolation procedures prior to mechanical work.
