# Technical Diagnosis Manual: Mechanical Seal Face Degradation & Seal Flush Loss

**Document ID:** KB-PUMP-011  
**Target Asset:** Industrial Centrifugal Slurry / Water Pump  
**Subsystem:** Stuffing Box & Cartridge Mechanical Seal Assembly  
**Severity Level:** High (Environmental Contamination & Bearing Contamination Risk)

---

## 1. Diagnostic Symptoms & Sensor Telemetry Profile
- **Physical Fluid Leakage:** Continuous liquid or slurry dripping/spraying from the seal gland drain port onto the drip tray.
- **Discharge Pressure:** Normal steady operation (**4.2 – 5.6 bar**). Major system pressure is maintained despite local seal weeping.
- **Vibration Velocity RMS:** Normal baseline (**1.2 – 1.8 mm/s**) or slight acoustic high-frequency seal face chattering squeal.
- **Bearing Temperature:** Normal (**50°C – 58°C**), unless leaking fluid enters the drive-end bearing bracket causing oil contamination.

---

## 2. Physical Root Causes
1. **Dry Running / Loss of Seal Flush:** API Plan 11/32/54 external seal flush flow interrupted, causing carbon/silicon-carbide seal faces to run dry and overheat.
2. **Thermal Shock Cracking:** Sudden cold liquid injection onto hot ceramic seal faces causing thermal stress fracture.
3. **Slurry Particle Embedding:** Abrasive solids migrating across the narrow fluid film between stationary and rotating seal faces.
4. **Secondary O-ring Degradation:** Chemical attack or thermal hardening of dynamic elastomeric O-rings.

---

## 3. Immediate Diagnostic Verification Checks
1. **Gland Drain Inspection:** Check rate of leakage from gland quench/drain port (more than 10 drops per minute indicates face failure).
2. **Seal Flush Plan Verification:** Check pressure gauge and flow rotameter on external seal flush line; verify flush pressure is at least 1.5 bar above stuffing box pressure.
3. **Acoustic Seal Listening:** Listen near the seal chamber with a contact probe for characteristic high-pitched squealing (indicates dry face rubbing).
4. **Bearing Oil Inspection:** Check drive-end bearing oil sight glass; milky white oil confirms seal leakage has breached the bearing isolator.

---

## 4. Recommended Corrective Maintenance Actions
1. **Restore Seal Flush:** Immediately clear flush line strainers, open throttle valves, and verify positive seal flush liquid flow.
2. **Cartridge Seal Replacement:** During scheduled shutdown, remove worn cartridge seal and install pre-set OEM cartridge seal.
3. **Shaft Sleeve Inspection:** Inspect shaft sleeve for fretting corrosion or grooving under the dynamic O-ring; replace sleeve if grooved.
4. **Oil Change:** If water entered bearing housing, immediately drain, flush, and refill bearing reservoir with fresh oil.

---

## 5. Safety Warnings & LOTO Notes
- **DANGER:** Chemical and biological hazard! Pumped fluid may be hazardous, acidic, toxic, or scalding hot. Wear chemical-resistant splash PPE.
- **LOTO:** Depressurize seal chamber and flush lines before loosening gland nuts.
