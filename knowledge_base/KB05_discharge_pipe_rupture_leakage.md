# Technical Diagnosis Manual: Discharge Pipe Rupture & Severe Flange Leakage

**Document ID:** KB-PUMP-005  
**Target Asset:** Industrial Centrifugal Slurry / Water Pump  
**Subsystem:** Hydraulic Discharge Piping & Flange Assembly  
**Severity Level:** Critical (Major Loss of Containment & Flooding Hazard)

---

## 1. Diagnostic Symptoms & Sensor Telemetry Profile
- **Discharge Pressure:** Drops abruptly below **3.0 bar**, falling down to **1.5 – 2.4 bar** (Severe collapse in developed system head).
- **Discharge Pressure Rate of Fall:** Rapid drop exceeding **-0.4 bar per second**.
- **Temperature:** Normal steady baseline (**48.0°C – 56.0°C**). No thermal warning triggered.
- **Vibration Velocity RMS:** Low to moderate (**1.2 – 2.0 mm/s**); mechanical rotating assembly is intact.
- **Motor Current:** Noticeable decrease in motor amperage due to loss of hydraulic backpressure resistance.

---

## 2. Physical Root Causes
1. **Flange Gasket Blowout:** Spiral-wound or elastomeric flange gasket sheared or extruded under pressure.
2. **Piping Corrosion-Erosion Breach:** Thinning of pipe wall near discharge spool or elbow rupture from abrasive slurry erosion.
3. **Open Dump / Bypass Valve:** Unintended opening of an upstream pressure relief valve, recirculation bypass, or manual drain valve.
4. **Sheared Expansion Joint:** Flexible rubber or metallic bellows expansion joint ruptured under piping stress.

---

## 3. Immediate Diagnostic Verification Checks
1. **Visual Plant Walkdown:** Immediately inspect the discharge piping corridor for liquid spray, pooling, steam clouds, or visible line rupture.
2. **Upstream Valve Alignment:** Check status of manual drain valves, bypass valves, and drain manifolds on the discharge header.
3. **Flowmeter Comparison:** Compare pump discharge flowmeter reading against downstream receiver tanks; discrepancy confirms line breach.
4. **Motor Load Check:** Verify if motor kilowatt consumption has dropped corresponding to the lack of backpressure.

---

## 4. Recommended Corrective Maintenance Actions
1. **Emergency Pump Stop:** Immediately trip pump from the local control station or DCS to halt uncontrolled fluid discharge.
2. **Isolate Piping Section:** Close motorized suction and discharge isolation valves to contain line inventory.
3. **Gasket & Pipe Replacement:** Replace blown gasket with rated Class 150/300 metallic gasket. Replace eroded pipe spools.
4. **Hydrostatic Testing:** Re-torque flange bolts in cross-pattern to specified torque; perform hydro-test prior to restarting pump.

---

## 5. Safety Warnings & LOTO Notes
- **DANGER:** High pressure fluid spray hazard! Slurry or hot water spray can cause severe lacerations, burns, and plant flooding.
- **LOTO:** Close, chain, and padlock suction and discharge block valves; depressurize and drain line prior to flange disassembly.
