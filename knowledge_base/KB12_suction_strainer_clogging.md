# Technical Diagnosis Manual: Suction Basket Strainer Debris Clogging

**Document ID:** KB-PUMP-012  
**Target Asset:** Industrial Centrifugal Slurry / Water Pump  
**Subsystem:** Intake Suction Piping & Basket Strainer  
**Severity Level:** Medium to High (Precursor to Full Cavitation)

---

## 1. Diagnostic Symptoms & Sensor Telemetry Profile
- **Suction Vacuum Gauge:** High negative suction pressure (deep vacuum reading, typically dropping below **-0.5 bar gauge**).
- **Differential Pressure Across Strainer:** High $\Delta P$ across the inlet basket strainer exceeding **0.8 bar**.
- **Discharge Pressure:** Gradual reduction in developed pressure (**3.2 – 4.0 bar**), accompanied by fluctuating delivery flow.
- **Vibration & Temperature:** Mild early cavitation turbulence (**1.8 – 2.4 mm/s**); bearing temperature remains normal (**48°C – 55°C**).

---

## 2. Physical Root Causes
1. **Debris Accumulation:** Leaves, plastic rags, plastic debris, or thick mineral sediment choking the perforated stainless steel strainer mesh.
2. **Marine Growth / Algae:** Biofouling or algae blooms clinging to intake screens on raw surface water intake systems.
3. **Improper Mesh Sizing:** Overly fine filter mesh installed on viscous or slurry fluid service.
4. **Upstream Sump Low Level:** Drawing sediment from the floor of an uncleaned suction pit.

---

## 3. Immediate Diagnostic Verification Checks
1. **Strainer Differential Gauge:** Read local $\Delta P$ manometer or differential pressure transmitter across the strainer housing.
2. **Suction Pressure Reading:** Confirm high vacuum condition at pump suction nozzle gauge.
3. **Pump Flowmeter Reading:** Check magnetic flowmeter for decreasing flow rate while pump RPM is unchanged.
4. **Casing Sound Check:** Check if early intermittent gravel noise is audible at the impeller eye.

---

## 4. Recommended Corrective Maintenance Actions
1. **Switch to Dual Duplex Strainer:** If equipped with a duplex strainer arrangement, switch the diverter lever to the clean basket chamber.
2. **Backwash / Clean Basket:** Isolate, drain, and remove the clogged strainer basket; pressure-wash with clean water and scrape mesh.
3. **Inspect Sump Intake:** Clear floating debris barriers and clean suction pit bottom.
4. **Mesh Verification:** Ensure basket perforations match fluid particulate sizing guidelines without creating excessive head loss.

---

## 5. Safety Warnings & LOTO Notes
- **CAUTION:** Never open a strainer cover without first confirming isolation and venting residual line pressure or vacuum.
- **LOTO:** Lock out inlet and outlet block valves before loosening strainer clamp bolts.
