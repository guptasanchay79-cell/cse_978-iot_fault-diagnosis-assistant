# Technical Diagnosis Manual: Pump-Motor Shaft Angular & Parallel Misalignment

**Document ID:** KB-PUMP-010  
**Target Asset:** Industrial Centrifugal Pump & Electric Motor Drive  
**Subsystem:** Flexible Shaft Coupling & Foundation Alignment  
**Severity Level:** Medium (Accelerates Bearing and Seal Failure)

---

## 1. Diagnostic Symptoms & Sensor Telemetry Profile
- **Vibration Characteristics:** Elevated **axial vibration** and radial vibration (**3.5 – 5.2 mm/s**).
- **Spectral Characteristic:** Dominant **2X running frequency** (two times shaft speed) component accompanied by 1X, with 180-degree phase shift across the flexible coupling.
- **Bearing Housing Temperature:** Mild symmetric rise on both pump drive-end and motor drive-end bearings (**60°C – 66°C**).
- **Discharge Pressure:** Normal steady operation (**4.5 – 5.5 bar**).

---

## 2. Physical Root Causes
1. **Thermal Growth Offset:** Inadequate cold alignment offset to compensate for thermal expansion between motor and pump during operation.
2. **Soft Foot Condition:** Uneven baseplate contact or loose anchor bolts creating structural foot twist when tightened.
3. **Piping Strain:** Pipe weight resting on pump suction/discharge nozzles pulling pump casing out of alignment.
4. **Coupling Element Deterioration:** Worn elastomeric spider or grid coupling insert failing to absorb residual angular deviation.

---

## 3. Immediate Diagnostic Verification Checks
1. **Cross-Coupling Vibration Phase:** Measure vibration phase across coupling; 180-degree phase difference in axial/radial direction confirms misalignment.
2. **Coupling Guard Thermography:** Infrared scan of the coupling guard; high localized friction heat indicates coupling flexing under strain.
3. **Laser Alignment Check:** Stop unit and immediately measure hot alignment with a precision dual-laser alignment system.
4. **Piping Strain Test:** Loosen pump flange bolts while monitoring dial indicators on the shaft; shaft deflection > 0.05 mm indicates pipe strain.

---

## 4. Recommended Corrective Maintenance Actions
1. **Precision Laser Re-alignment:** Realign pump and motor shafts to within $\pm 0.05\text{ mm}$ parallel and $\pm 0.05\text{ mm/100mm}$ angular tolerance.
2. **Shim Adjustment:** Use stainless steel pre-cut shims under motor feet to eliminate soft foot before final torquing.
3. **Piping Hanger Adjustment:** Adjust pipe hangers and spring supports to eliminate external nozzle loads.
4. **Replace Coupling Element:** Inspect and replace worn elastomeric coupling inserts or lubricate metallic gear couplings.

---

## 5. Safety Warnings & LOTO Notes
- **WARNING:** Never run machinery with coupling guards removed. High-speed rotating shafts present severe entanglement hazards.
- **LOTO:** Follow standard electrical lockout procedures prior to removing coupling guards.
